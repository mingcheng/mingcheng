I'm mingcheng (明城), an open-source practitioner from Ningbo, now living in Hangzhou, China. I love building useful software and working with the communities that keep it thriving.

I'm currently a senior software architect and open-source evangelist at [Ant Group](https://github.com/antgroup). Before that, I spent nearly a decade at [NetEase](https://www.netease.com/) as a senior technical lead and team manager.

My background is in cloud-native systems and software architecture, and lately I've been focusing on AI agents and infrastructure. I care about clean design, reliable automation, and turning good ideas into systems that actually work.

Linux is my go-to: Debian for servers and containers, and [Kalpa Desktop](https://kalpadesktop.org/) on the desktop. I also use macOS for development and testing.

## 🛠️ Personal Projects

In my spare time, I build and maintain a few small tools for everyday development and terminal use:

- [aigitcommit](https://github.com/mingcheng/aigitcommit) — Generate Conventional Commit messages with OpenAI-compatible APIs.
- [mp3blstr](https://github.com/mingcheng/mp3blstr) — Play music with a terminal player inspired by mp3blaster.
- [simplyddns](https://github.com/mingcheng/simplyddns) — Keep DNS records up to date with configurable IP sources and DNS providers.
- [socks5lb](https://github.com/mingcheng/socks5lb) — Balance traffic across SOCKS5 proxies with automatic health checks.
- [stupidict](https://github.com/mingcheng/stupidict) — Look up English words offline in an English-Chinese terminal dictionary.

They're all available from my [Homebrew tap](https://github.com/mingcheng/homebrew-tap): `brew install mingcheng/tap/<package>`. You can find more projects on my [GitHub repositories](https://github.com/mingcheng?tab=repositories) page.
